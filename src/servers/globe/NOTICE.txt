Server globe: local, simplified geographic data and projection engine.
Natural Earth 5.1.2 countries (1:50m): public domain.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
Simplification tolerance 0.035 degrees; coordinates rounded to 0.001 degrees.
Map outlines are for server location selection, not precise boundary reference.
d3-array 3.2.4 and d3-geo 3.1.1: ISC; licenses accompany these files.
d3-array embeds internmap (InternMap/InternSet), also ISC; see LICENSE-internmap.txt.
Rebuild: node scripts/build-server-globe.cjs --download
